WHAT A RENDER ACTUALLY COSTS

One scene.

Real numbers.

Bananafarm doesn't run a render farm, it connects you to Vast.ai and RunPod, and you pay them directly at their rates. Here's what a 120-frame animation costs through it.

Standard runs on standard GPUs. Secure runs on datacentre GPUs with claimed secure storage. Each figure averages an RTX 4090 and an RTX 3090, over five runs.

Bananafarm connects your machine to third-party cloud GPU platforms. It does not host, operate or control that hardware. Files you upload go to the provider you choose, and the privacy, security and performance of that infrastructure are governed by that provider's terms, not ours. Costs shown are indicative and vary with GPU availability, region, queue and provider pricing.

HOW IT WORKS

From Blender to final render.

No command line. No browser uploads. Just render.

01

Connect your cloud GPU

Connect the Bananafarm app to your favourite cloud GPU provider.

02

Upload & render from Blender

Upload your scene and send the render directly to the cloud without leaving Blender.

03

Download your renders

Finished frames download straight to your machine as soon as the render completes.

Your scene stays in Blender. Bananafarm takes care of the render.
